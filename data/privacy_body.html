Privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026. This policy covers chromachaos.kafkami.nl and the ChromaChaos Pinterest integration.

Who we are

ChromaChaos is an independent digital art studio based in the Netherlands, selling watercolour clipart through our Etsy shop. Contact: send a message through our Etsy shop.

This website

This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for a short period for security and reliability; we do not use them to identify visitors.

Purchases

All purchases happen on Etsy, and Etsy's own privacy policy applies to them. Etsy shares with us the order details needed to deliver your download and answer support questions. We use them only for that, and we never sell or share them.

Our Pinterest integration

We use a private application ("ChromaPin") that connects only to our own Pinterest business account. It publishes pins of our own products to our own boards and reads statistics about our own pins and boards. It does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user. The access token Pinterest issues to it is stored securely on our own computer, is never shared or sold, and can be revoked at any time from our Pinterest account settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by sending a message through our Etsy shop. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
